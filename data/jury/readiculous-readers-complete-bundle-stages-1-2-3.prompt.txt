Learning Goals Master phonics from first sounds right through to advanced spelling patterns, in one seamless set. What's Inside All 3 stages of Readiculous Readers — 36 expertly sequenced, fully decodable paperback books in total. Stage 1 (12 books): introduces vowel digraphs and consonant blends. Stage 3 (12 books): tackles advanced spelling patterns (ay, ie, wh) and split digraphs (a-e, i-e). Hilarious stories starring Mrs Wordsmith's much-loved cast of characters throughout. One complete phonics journey, so there's no guessing what to buy next. How It Works Each stage introduces new phonics patterns in context, through fully decodable words and sentences. Children move from sounding out single words to reading whole, funny stories independently — at their own pace. Confidence Building: children decode increasingly complex words independently, boosting self-esteem with every page turn.
